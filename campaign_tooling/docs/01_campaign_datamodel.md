General Purpose Mail/Email Campaign Manager
====

Overview
===
Provide a re-usable structure for offering up campaigns including a purpose/intent, a set of actions typically including contacting relevant people by mail, email, or fax, and tracking the responses.

Components
===
The components of the project include
* Campaign
* Respondent

Campaign
===
The elements of the campaign are:
* internal ID
* Name (short title for UI purposes)
* Description (blurb, typically paragraph-sized)
* Full-body text (a rich, HTML or Markdown set of copy that explains the campaign in more detail)
* Active?

Campaigns also have a set of Campaign Targets and Campaign Artifacts

Campaign Targets
===
The individual recipients or targets of the campaign. The details of the Campaign Targets are:
* Formal Name (Governor Maura Healey)
* First Name (Maura)
* Last Name (Healey)
* Salutation (Governor Healey)
* Email: (gov@mass.gov)
* Physical Address: (1 State House Dr., Boston MA 02001)

Campaign Artifacts
===
Campaign artifacts are the raw materials that will be sent to the campaign recipients. Initially the artifacts are primarily documents that will be printed or emailed. They need some basic parameter substitution to do things like insert the letter-writer’s contact information, and the campaign target’s name, title, and contact information.
