## Understanding Your Role

| Role | What Your Button Says | What Happens | Who Reviews |
|------|----------------------|--------------|-------------|
| **News Team** | "PUBLISH STORY" | Story goes live immediately | No review needed |
| **News Contributor** | "SUBMIT FOR REVIEW" | Story saved as "Pending" | News Team or Admin approves |

**News Team Members:** Christine, Steve  
**News Contributors:** Submit → Needs approval before publishing

## How to Post a Story

### Step 1: Go to the Portal
Visit: **https://lexingtonalarm.org/post-news/**

### Step 2: Log In
Enter your username and password. Click "Forgot Password?" if needed.

### Step 3: Fill In the Form

| Field | Required? | Tips |
|-------|-----------|------|
| **Story Title** | ✅ Yes | Clear, descriptive headline |
| **Byline** | No | Defaults to your name. Change for reprints only |
| **Upload Image** | No | Recommended! Check "Use as Featured Image" |
| **Story Summary** | No | 2-3 sentences for the news card preview |
| **Full Story** | ✅ Yes | Your complete article |
| **Category** | ✅ Yes | Choose one (see Featured Story section below) |

### Step 4: Choose Category

- **Blog Post** = Appears in the "Recent News" grid
- **Featured Story** = Appears prominently at the TOP of the News page (see Featured Story section below)

### Step 5: Submit

- **News Team** sees **"PUBLISH STORY"** → Goes live immediately
- **News Contributor** sees **"SUBMIT FOR REVIEW"** → Waits for approval

## Editing Stories

Both published stories and the featured summary text can be edited at any time from our news portal. Click on the **Edit** link for the story you want to edit.

In the **Manage Recent Posts** section at the bottom of the portal, you'll see:
- List of recent stories with Edit and View links
- Click **Edit** to modify any story's title, content, summary, or category
- Changes save immediately

---

## Featured Story

The Featured Story appears at the top of our News section and also on our Home page. Generally, Toby determines which story should be featured.

**If you need to change the featured story:**

Simply check the "Featured Story" box when posting a new story. The system automatically handles the rest—the previous featured story will be moved to a standard blog post automatically.

**Note:** Only ONE story displays as featured at a time. When you mark a new story as Featured, the old featured story is automatically demoted to Blog Posts.

---

## Photos

All our posts are better with photos! Use the image upload to add a photo to your story.

**Before uploading, make sure:**
- You have permission to use the photo, OR
- The photo is in the public domain (e.g., from Wikipedia, government sources, or similar)

**To add a photo:**
1. Click **"Choose File"** in the Upload Image field
2. Select your image file
3. Check **"Use as Featured Image"** to make it the main photo for the story
4. The image will display with your published story

## What Happens After You Submit

### For News Team Members (Immediate Publish)
1. Story goes live on the News page
2. All team members receive an email notification
3. Done!

### For News Contributors (Pending Review)
1. Story saved as "Pending Review"
2. **Email notification sent** to News Team editors
3. A News Team member reviews your story
4. They approve → Story goes live
5. Or they may contact you for edits

---

## How to Review & Approve Pending Stories
*(For News Team members only)*

1. Click the link in the notification email
2. Review the story content
3. Make any edits needed using the Edit link
4. Change status to Published

## Managing Your Posts

Below the submission form, you'll see **"Manage Recent Posts"**:

- ✅ Check boxes to select posts
- ✏️ Click "Edit" to modify a story
- 👁️ Click "View" to see published story
- 🗑️ "Delete Selected" removes checked posts

**What you can manage depends on your role:**
- **News Team**: All team posts
- **News Contributor**: Only your own posts

## Auto-Save Feature

Your draft saves automatically as you type!

- Look for: **"✓ Draft auto-saved"** message
- Gray **"SAVE DRAFT"** button for manual save
- If your browser crashes, your draft should be recoverable when you return

---

## Email Notifications

**Everyone on the News Team receives an email when:**
- Any new story is submitted (pending or published)
- Subject line tells you if review is needed:
  - `[NEEDS REVIEW]` = Pending approval
  - `[New Story Published]` = Already live
