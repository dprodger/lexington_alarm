# Lexington Alarm Media System
## Project Overview for Team

**Date:** January 10, 2026  
**Project Lead:** Toby

---

## What We're Building

We're creating a new **MEDIA** section on lexingtonalarm.org to serve as the organization's visual archive. This will be a professional, searchable collection of our best photos and videos - the visual record of our movement.

The MEDIA section will have two main areas:

### 📷 Photos
A curated photo gallery organized into albums by event, date, or campaign. Think of it as our official photo archive - the best shots from rallies, standouts, meetings, and community actions.

**Features:**
- Album organization (e.g., "ICE OUT FOR GOOD Standout - January 10, 2026")
- Each album has a title, date, location, and description
- Individual photos can have captions
- Search across all photos
- Fast loading (images load as you scroll, not all at once)
- Beautiful lightbox viewing

### 🎬 Videos
An organized archive of our video content - speaker recordings, event footage, and social media clips.

**Features:**
- Organized by category: Speakers, Events, Social Clips, My Talks
- Pulls from our YouTube channel (no duplicate hosting)
- Searchable by speaker name, event, or topic
- Videos only load when you click play (keeps the site fast)

---

## Who Can Upload

A small team of 3-5 authorized members will have upload access for photos. This keeps the collection curated and high-quality rather than a free-for-all.

If you'd like to contribute photos, send them to the designated uploaders who will add them to the appropriate albums.

## Technology

**Photos:** We're using Envira Gallery, a professional WordPress gallery plugin used by organizations like National Geographic and the NBA. It's built for performance and handles large photo collections well.

**Videos:** Custom integration with our YouTube channel. Videos are stored on YouTube (where they already live) but displayed beautifully on our site with smart loading that doesn't slow down page performance.
