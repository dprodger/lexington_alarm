Get Involved with Lexington Alarm

Lexington Alarm! is entirely volunteer-driven. We have committees for ongoing work and an active volunteer list for actions like tabling, selling signs, joining standouts, and connecting with local immigrant-rights groups.

Check the activities that interest you. Our visible resistance to tyranny depends on people who can help weekly, biweekly, or occasionally. By signing up, you'll receive updates about actions, projects, and committee opportunities, and our bi-weekly newsletter.
